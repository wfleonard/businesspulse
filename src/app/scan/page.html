Inside the scan

What BusinessPulse checks

  • AI

    AI answer visibility

    We ask the questions your customers ask AI assistants and record whether your business is named, how it is described, and who gets named instead.

  • SRP

    Search ranking

    Where you place for the local searches that bring in paying customers, compared with nearby competitors.

  • SCH

    Structured data

    Whether your site tells search engines and AI tools, in their own format, what you do, where you are and when you are open.

  • HLT

    Site health

    Speed, mobile layout, broken links, security and the technical issues that quietly push you down the list.

  • GBP

    Listings and reviews

    Consistency of your name, address and phone across Google and directories, plus the review signals that build trust.

  • FIX

    Prioritized fixes

    A plain English list of what to fix first, ranked by the return you are likely to see.
