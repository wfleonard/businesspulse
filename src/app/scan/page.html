What BusinessPulse checks

    AI answer visibility

    We ask AI search 20 questions your buyers ask, chosen for your industry and town, and record whether each answer cites your website.

  • NAM

    Named or linked

    Whether an answer links to your site, names your business without a link, or only finds you through a directory listing.

    Who gets cited instead

    The competitors, directories like Yelp and Angi, and guides AI search sends buyers to when it doesn't send them to you.

    Results by type of question

    Prioritized fixes

    A plain English list of what to fix first, ranked by the return you are likely to see.
