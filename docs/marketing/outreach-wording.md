# Answer card outreach wording

Wording to send with an answer card. Fill in the brackets from the snapshot run.
Use a Square card with real names for a one-to-one message, and a blurred card
for anything public.

Pick a question labeled "Not mentioned" that cites 3 or 4 sites, and quote it
exactly as it appears on the card.

---

## 1. Cold email (you ran the snapshot, they didn't ask)

Attach: Square card with real names.

```
Subject: Asked AI about [trade] near [town]

Hi [First name],

I asked Perplexity "[question from the card]" this week. The answer pointed
buyers to [3 or 4] websites, and [Business] wasn't one of them. Screenshot
attached.

That was 1 of 20 buyer questions I ran for your trade and area. Want the other
19? Reply yes and I'll send your free snapshot.

Best,

Bill Leonard
Saxon Enterprises, Inc.
(732) 673-4260
wfleonard@saxonenterprises.net

Not interested? Reply "no thanks" and I won't write again.
Saxon Enterprises, Inc., [street address], Tinton Falls, NJ [ZIP]
```

The last two lines are there for CAN-SPAM, which requires an opt-out and a
postal address in cold commercial email. Fill in the street address once and
keep it.

## 2. Follow-up (5 to 7 days later, no reply)

Reply on the same thread so the card is still in it.

```
Hi [First name],

Following up on the AI answer I sent last week. The offer stands: 20 buyer
questions for [trade] in [town], free, and you'll see who gets named instead
of you.

Reply yes and it's on its way.

Thanks,
Peace Bill
```

## 4. LinkedIn connection request (300 character limit)

Connection requests can't carry an image. Send the card after they accept.

```
Hi [Name], I asked AI for [trade] near [town] and looked at which businesses it sent buyers to. Ran the same check on [Business]. Happy to share what it found, no cost. — Bill Leonard, Saxon Enterprises
```

About 215 characters with short fill-ins. Keep the filled-in version under 300.

If your business wasn't one of them, that buyer never saw you.

---

## Rules for all of these

- Quote the question exactly as it appears on the card. Don't paraphrase.
- Name only what the card shows: which sites one AI answer cited. Don't
  mention Google rank, site health, listings or AI Overviews.
- Free snapshot: 20 questions, Perplexity. Pulse: $100 per report, plus NJ
  sales tax for New Jersey buyers.
- Keep blurred cards for anything public. Real names go only to the business
  the card is about.
